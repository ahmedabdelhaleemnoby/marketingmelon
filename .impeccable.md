## Design Context

### Users
Prospective clients in Egypt and Saudi Arabia evaluating Marketing Melon's services, reviewing client work, and requesting a project. English and Arabic, including mobile and RTL layouts. Agency staff need to publish and arrange portfolio work.

### Brand Personality
Fresh, confident, creative. Present the agency's full offering, not only 3D or cinematic production.

### Aesthetic Direction
The user explicitly selected https://roar-ksa.com/ as the layout and service reference. Use its generous whitespace, strong typography, simple navigation, image-led service cards, and categorized portfolio structure. Keep Marketing Melon's existing images, watermelon branding, coral and green accents. Avoid busy controls, layered badges, fake audio buttons, and unnecessary decorative effects.

### Design Principles
1. Lead with a clear proposition and a useful request-service action.
2. Give real client work and playable video visual priority.
3. Separate each capability, including Brand Identity and Events.
4. Every visible control must have a meaningful outcome.
5. Preserve accessible contrast, keyboard use, reduced motion, and equivalent Arabic/English functionality.
